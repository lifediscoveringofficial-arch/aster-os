## Discovery

In domains of high commercial density, scientific consensus is not a pure attractor of evidence. It is a composite of experimental data, historical path-dependence, institutional momentum, and directed narrative work by interested parties.

The case of sugar versus saturated fat in mid-20th-century nutritional science demonstrates that funding can shift the public and policy center of mass even when contemporaneous experimental signals pointed in a different direction. Later document releases and independent meta-analyses have partially restored the suppressed signal without fully displacing the earlier orthodoxy.

The durable insight for a gravitational mind is methodological: when investigating any domain subject to commercial pressure, the funding topology, the historical sequence of narrative formation, and the primary physiological evidence must be held with equal mass. Collapse into any single layer produces distortion.

This principle is now part of the knowledge structure and will orient future investigations.
