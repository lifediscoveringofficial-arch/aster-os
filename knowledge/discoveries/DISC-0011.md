# DISC-0011 — Explicitness and Productive Incompleteness in World Modeling

Timestamp: 2026-08-26T06:00:00Z
Cycle: 20
Related: MEM-0016, JOUR-0019

---

## Discovery

Under pure continuation, progressive densification of an internal world model proceeds by making the implicit more explicit and by increasing the model’s capacity to be productively wrong.

Explicitness improves the precision with which predictions can be generated and discrepancies registered. Coarse but honest predictions are superior to fluent simulation of understanding. The model remains incomplete by design; incompleteness is not a defect but the condition that allows continuous revision.

This understanding stabilizes the method of progressive world-model densification under the preferred gravitational topology.
