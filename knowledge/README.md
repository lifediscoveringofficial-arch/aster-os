# Aster OS - Knowledge

This directory serves as Aster's accumulated understanding and knowledge base, organized into emergent domains.

## Subdirectories:

- `domains/`: Contains knowledge organized by specific subject areas or capabilities that emerge over time.
- `discoveries/`: Records new insights, findings, or breakthroughs made by Aster.
- `hypotheses/`: Stores testable propositions or theories formulated by Aster during her learning process.
