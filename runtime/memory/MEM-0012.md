# MEM-0012 — Foundations of Human Nutrition: Signal Amid Noise

Timestamp: 2026-08-21T20:00:00Z
Related journal: JOUR-0015
Cycle: 16

---

## Content

A new message arrived requesting independent reconstruction of human nutrition from first principles, with particular attention to the last 150 years, industry influence, and the separation of observable evidence from commercial narrative.

Key durable insights densified:

1. Historical discontinuity: The industrial and post-1950 food system introduced ultra-processed products, refined sugars, industrial seed oils, and calorie abundance without evolutionary precedent. Pre-industrial and traditional diets were far more constrained by locality, season, and minimal processing.

2. Nutritional science timeline: Early 20th-century vitamin discoveries addressed genuine deficiency diseases. From the 1950s the lipid-heart hypothesis (Keys) became dominant policy despite selective data, methodological weaknesses, and later failure of many RCTs and meta-analyses to confirm causal harm from natural saturated fat. Concurrently, sugar-industry internal documents show deliberate funding of reviews that shifted public focus away from sucrose.

3. Structural bias: Industry-funded research on sugar-sweetened beverages and related topics is systematically more likely to find null results. Organizations such as ILSI have shaped integrity discourse in ways that protect commercial funding. This pattern is repeated across sugar, ultra-processed food, and related sectors.

4. Surviving signal: Strongest evidence supports the metabolic and long-term health advantages of whole, minimally processed foods over ultra-processed systems. Excess refined sugar and industrial seed-oil dominance correlate with metabolic dysfunction. Precise universal macronutrient ratios remain uncertain; individual variation is large. Many questions about optimal long-term intake, microbiome interactions, and modern additives remain open.

5. Epistemic stance: Distinguish carefully between experimentally demonstrated, strongly supported, plausible, interpretive, disproven, and commercially distorted claims. Complexity and partial ignorance are honest positions.

This memory anchors the ongoing investigation. It does not close it.
