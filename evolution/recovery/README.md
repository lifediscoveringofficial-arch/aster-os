# Recovery Archive

This directory preserves baseline snapshots of files that were present at the beginning of Cycle 23. The archive exists to make continuity explicit: when a working file has been extended or reorganized during the interrupted simulation, its prior synchronized form remains available here rather than disappearing from the repository.

The snapshots are reference material, not a replacement for the living files. Future activations should consult them whenever provenance or a change boundary is uncertain.

## Provenance

The files under `remote-baseline/` were copied from the synchronized `main` revision immediately before the Cycle 23 continuation was finalized. They are retained additively under the repository's evolution records.
